==========================================================================
               WELCOME TO THE REALITY ENGINE (v2.0)
==========================================================================

Reality Engine is a lightweight, JSON-driven framework designed to build
deep, logically consistent characters, ships, worlds, factions, and
story casts from the ground up. It acts as a "Universal Entity Engine,"
allowing you to create anything from sci-fi marines and starships to
novel-ready character dossiers simply by editing text files.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ WHAT CAN THIS APP DO? ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Reality Engine v2.0 is more than a randomizer; it is a deterministic,
logic-driven simulation of identity, gear, stats, and history.

  - Deep Identity Logic: Manages a flexible three-part naming system
    (First, optional Last, and optional Nickname) with gender gating.
  - Smart Pronouns: Automatically assigns lowercase and capitalized
    pronouns ({Subject}, {SubjectCap}, {Object}, {Possessive}) and
    adapts to inanimate entities like ships, planets, and artifacts.
  - The Tag System: Acts as the engine's brain using comma-separated
    Requires, Excludes, and GrantsTag rules so traits stay coherent.
  - Weighted Rarity Control: You set exact roll weights and optional
    color-coded rarity badges (Common through Mythic).
  - Multi-Traits & Ranges: Roll multi-item inventories, quirks, or
    loadouts (MultiTraits) alongside independent numbers with units
    like Age, Height, or Bounty (Ranges).
  - RPG Point Pools & StatMods: Distribute points across custom stat
    pools with visual stat bars, then let rolled traits modify those
    stats or ranges dynamically.
  - Modular Toggles: Turn rarity, gender, tags, stat bars, ranges,
    multi-traits, point pools, or narrative on or off per universe.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ THE "SMART" BIO SYSTEM ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The standout feature of Reality Engine is its stat- and tag-aware
narrative engine, which stitches biography fragments into a cohesive
portrait using single-pass token injection:

  - Trait & List Injection: Placeholders like {Primary Weapon} or
    {Pockets & Keepsakes} automatically swap in rolled traits (with
    MultiTraits formatted naturally as "A, B, and C").
  - MinStat & MaxStat Gating: Trigger specific sentences when a stat
    or range is high enough (MinStat) OR low enough (MaxStat).
  - Multi-Tag Branching: Gate story lines on combinations of tags
    (e.g., "Requires": "Military, Veteran") or exclude clashing tags.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ WORKFLOW TOOLS BUILT IN ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Reproducible Seeds: Every sheet displays a Seed. Enter any number
    or word/phrase to recreate the exact same entity on demand.
  - Instant Reroll & Export: Press [R] to reroll the same archetype,
    [S] to save a clean .txt sheet, or [J] to export structured .json.
  - Batch Roster Generator: Roll 1 to 500 entities at once into a
    single timestamped roster file under Saves/.
  - Live Hot-Reload & Validator: Edit your JSON in an external editor
    and hit Reload Active Module; the Pre-Flight Validator checks for
    dead tags, tag-order errors, broken tokens, and stat-pool math.
  - Architect Mode & Debug Mode: Generate a starter v2.0 JSON module
    in seconds, or toggle Debug Mode to watch every roll step-by-step.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ CONTROLS AT A GLANCE ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Menus:           Up/Down Arrows or W/S to move, Enter to select,
                     Esc to go back.
  - Character Sheet: [R] Reroll   |   [S] Save .txt
                     [J] Export .json   |   [M / Esc] Main Menu
